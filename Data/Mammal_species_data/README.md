# Data - mammal species

1.  **`mammal_threat_data.csv`**: Anthropogenic threat data downloaded from the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) database.
2.  **`mammal_traits_n_threat_data.csv`**: Anthropogenic threat and trait data used in logistic regression models.
3.  **`Phylacine_trait_data.csv`**: Mammal species trait data (i.e., diet functional groups, and body mass) obtained from the PHYLACINE database.
4.  **`Eltonian_trait_data.csv`**: Mammal species trait data (i.e., diet functional groups, and body mass) obtained from the ELTONIAN TRAITS database.
5.  **`Phylacine_synonyms.csv`**: Mammal species synonyms obtained from the PHYLACINE database.
6.  **`synonyms_mammals_iucn.csv`**: Mammal species synonyms obtained from the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) database.
7.  **`mammal_extinct_risk.csv`**: Mammal species conservation status obtained from the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) database.
