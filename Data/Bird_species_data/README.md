# Data - Bird species

1.  **`Aves_threat_data.csv`**: Anthropogenic threat data downloaded from the the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) database.
2.  **`Aves_threats_traits_data.csv`**: Combined anthropogenic threat and trait data used to logistic regression models.
3.  **`AVONET_BirdLife.csv`**: Bird species trait data (i.e., diet functional groups, and body mass) obtained from the AVONET database.
4.  **`Birds_extinct_risk.csv`**: Conservation status of bird species downloaded from from the the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) database.
